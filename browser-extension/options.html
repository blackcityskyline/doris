doris search bridge

Sends a title to doris, the torrent search TUI. The search runs in doris's own window; this add-on only hands it the title.

The port is bridge_port in ~/.config/doris/config.toml. Leave it empty for the default. 127.0.0.1 rather than localhost: where one resolves to ::1 and the daemon listens on 127.0.0.1 only, the other address reaches nothing.

Button on a page IMDb, Trakt, Kinopoisk and Lampa. Appears next to the title and says sent, refused or doris is not listening.
Toolbar button Searches the tab's title from any site, including ones with no button on the page.
Refused searches A page doris does not recognise is answered 403 origin not allowed and no search runs. That is deliberate: the bridge acts, so it answers an allow list rather than *.
